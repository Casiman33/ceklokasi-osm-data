CekLokasi conservatively treats each filtered, normalized OSM database published here as a Derivative Database. Reuse remains subject to the ODbL, including its attribution requirements.

Each immutable Release contains the database, machine-readable provenance metadata, and checksums. The public methodology describes the transformation at a level sufficient to understand the filtering, normalization, representative-coordinate, and within-OSM deduplication behavior.

Every snapshot activated in CekLokasi Production is kept publicly downloadable and is not deleted or overwritten during normal operation. Indefinite retention is CekLokasi's operational policy; it is not presented as an explicit ODbL retention period.

This notice describes CekLokasi's engineering compliance posture and is not a general legal opinion.
